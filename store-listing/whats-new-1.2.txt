Lifts now show a Mark done button wherever they appear on the schedule,
however your coach worded or tagged them — "Team Lift in Sherm" counts
the same as one tagged LIFT. Lifts later in the week show the button
greyed out until the day arrives. Marking one off updates your Lifts
this week count and shows in the coaches' lift attendance grid.

Your Goals can now be put in the order you want: drag a goal by the
handle on its left. The three you move to the top are the three shown
when the list is collapsed, and the order follows you to any device.
