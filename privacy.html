ScotSwim Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2, 2026

ScotSwim is a team app for Alma College's swimming and diving program — a roster, schedule, training log, and coach communication tool for its athletes and coaches. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who can see it.

Who this app is for

Access is limited to Alma College swimming and diving athletes and coaching staff. There is no public sign-up — every account is created from a coach-issued invite link tied to a specific email address and roster spot.

What we collect

Account info — your email address and the password you choose (handled entirely by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password ourselves), plus your role (athlete or coach) and which roster entry the account belongs to.

Times, scores, and training data you enter — swim times or dive scores you log under My Events, and training volume (miles/minutes) you log under Training. This is entered by you, voluntarily, and is never inferred or collected automatically.

Coach-authored content — goals and prescribed workouts a coach writes for an athlete, team announcements, and posted workout sheets (which may include a photo the coach attaches, e.g. a lift-day exercise sheet).

Practice attendance — taken by coaches per practice day. This is stored only on the coach's own device (browser local storage), not on our servers, and is not shared between devices or accounts.

Profile photo positioning — if you adjust how your profile photo is framed/cropped in the app, that adjustment is saved only on your own device, never uploaded anywhere.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, ad tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • No location tracking.
  • No public profiles — only people signed into a ScotSwim account (teammates and coaches) can see roster, times, and training data, never the general public.

Where data lives

Account and app data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication), a cloud database and login service. Some publicly available meet times and results are pulled in read-only from SwimCloud, a third-party swimming statistics site, purely for display — nothing from ScotSwim is sent back to SwimCloud.

Optional background app updates

The installed mobile app can receive content updates (not new native code, just refreshed app content) via Capgo, an over-the-air update service. This only delivers app updates — it does not collect personal data about you.

Your choices

  • You can stop entering times, training logs, or photo adjustments at any point — nothing is collected unless you actively enter it.
  • To have your account and its data deleted, contact us using the email below.

Children's privacy

ScotSwim is built for a college athletics program; it isn't directed at children, and accounts are only issued by a coach to enrolled college athletes and staff.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
