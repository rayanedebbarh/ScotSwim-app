New in this version:

• Notifications — you'll know when a coach posts a team announcement, changes the meet schedule, or sets a goal for you. Tap one to jump straight to it.

• Excused absences — coaches can mark an absence as excused or unexcused. An excused absence doesn't count against your attendance and doesn't break your practice streak.

• Your goals now follow your account, so they're waiting for you when you sign in on another device.

• Goals a coach sets for you now show up in your app.

Fixes:

• Goals no longer reorder themselves while you're adjusting them
• The rivalry card no longer gets stuck when you pick an event no teammate has logged yet
• Training log entries no longer overwrite each other while you type
• Tap anywhere outside a profile to close it, not just the X
• Tapping a teammate's photo opens their profile instead of asking you to upload a picture
